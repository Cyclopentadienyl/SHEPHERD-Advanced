# Sources behind the WebUI design rules

Collected on 2026-10-25 by five research passes (see `docs/working/WEBUI_UX_REVIEW_2026-10-25.md`). Only
sources that a rule or an implementation reference in that review cites are listed; each was opened, or seen in
search results where marked. A takeaway is a paraphrase, not a quote. Re-check a source before relying on a
detail that may have changed (Gradio behaviour is pinned to 6.30.0).

## Design guidance and design systems

- [NN/g – Progressive Disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/): Show core options first and defer the rest. Being on the first screen tells users an item is important. Designs deeper than 2 levels 'typically have low usability'. The control that reveals more must be obvious and labelled with strong information scent.…
- [NN/g – Tooltip Guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tooltip-guidelines/): 'Users shouldn't need to find a tooltip in order to complete their task.' Keep tips brief ('lengthy content is no longer a tip') and never redundant. Support keyboard hover. Never cover the content the tip describes. Use tooltips consistently or use popup…
- [NN/g – Flat UI Elements Attract Less Attention and Cause Uncertainty](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-ui-less-attention-cause-uncertainty/): With weak signifiers, tasks took about 22% longer and drew about 25% more fixations ('click uncertainty'). A static subheading was mistaken for a link. Dense pages suffer most from weak signifiers.
- [NN/g – Beyond Blue Links: Making Clickable Elements Recognizable](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/clickable-elements/): Don't style static items like links or buttons: 'giving headings a background color will make them resemble buttons'. Avoid elements that look clickable but aren't, and keep headings and labels visually separate from controls.
- [NN/g – Tabs, Used Right](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/): Show the selected tab with at least two indicators, never a thin 1px stroke. Keep tabs in one row above the panel, with labels of 1–2 words and no ALL CAPS. Never mix in-page tabs and navigation tabs in one control, and make the two kinds look different.…
- [NN/g – Visual Hierarchy in UX: Definition](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/): Use at most 3 type sizes, at most 2 large elements and at most 3 contrast variations ('If everything is contrasted, then nothing stands out'). Keep headings close to their content with larger gaps between groups, and use containers sparingly. Check with a…
- [NN/g – Dashboards: Making Charts and Graphs Easier to Understand (preattentive)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dashboards-preattentive/): A dashboard is an at-a-glance single screen, not an exploration tool. Encode quantities by length and 2D position (lines and bars). Don't use color for magnitude, and pair color with shape. Group related items by proximity and borders.
- [NN/g – Dropdown Lists](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dropdown-list/): Long dropdown lists are 'especially problematic'. Beyond about 15 options, consider a combobox; under 5, radio buttons are usually better. Show options as buttons when users need to compare them.
- [NN/g – Aesthetic and Minimalist Design (heuristic #8)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-minimalist-design/): 'Every extra unit of information in an interface competes with the relevant units of information.' Disclose progressively. Communicate, don't decorate. Don't overuse font or color variations.
- [NN/g – Minimize Cognitive Load to Maximize Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/minimize-cognitive-load/): 'Redundant links, irrelevant images, and meaningless typography flourishes slow users down.' Offload work by re-displaying information and setting smart defaults.
- [NN/g – 8 Design Guidelines for Complex Applications](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/complex-application-design/): Reduce clutter without reducing capability through staged disclosure. Ease the move between primary and secondary information without leaving the screen. Make important information salient by removing non-essential elements.
- [NN/g – Text Scanning Patterns: Eyetracking Evidence](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-scanning-patterns-eyetracking/): The layer-cake pattern (scanning headings and subheadings) is 'by far the most effective'. Bold keywords support the spotted pattern. Bullets and chunking help, and walls of text go unread.
- [NN/g – First 2 Words: A Signal for the Scanning Eye](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/first-2-words-a-signal-for-scanning/): Users see about 2 words (about 11 characters) of each list item. 35% of the links tested gave no clue from their first 11 characters. Front-load user- and action-oriented terms.
- [NN/g – Data Tables: Four Major User Tasks](https://nngroup.com/articles/data-tables) (search result only): Four core tasks: find records, compare, view/edit one row, act on records. Tables beat cards for comparison because adjacent values need no reorientation.
- [Carbon – Toggletip usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/toggletip/usage/): Opens on click, Enter or Space and closes on Esc or an outside click. Interactive elements are allowed. Maximum width 288px. When content overflows, only the body scrolls. Not for critical information.
- [Carbon – Tooltip usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/tooltip/usage/): For brief, non-interactive supplementary text only; never essential instructions (use helper text). No links or buttons inside. Definition tooltips suit labels and tables. Never cover the related content. Esc dismisses.
- [Carbon – Structured list usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/structured-list/usage/): Simple, read-only, term/definition-style data with no nesting. Column headers are 1–2 words. Comes in default and condensed sizes. Use a data table for complex or large data.
- [Carbon – Data table usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/): Row heights are 24/32/40/48/64px, and the header row matches the row size. Column titles are 1–2 words, wrapping to 2 lines and then truncating with the full title in a tooltip. Toolbar holds at most 5 actions. Zebra striping is optional. Title plus…
- [Carbon – UI shell left panel usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/UI-shell-left-panel/usage/): Use the left panel when there are more than 5 secondary navigation items. Two tiers at most; go deeper with in-page tabs.
- [Carbon – Tag usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/tag/usage/): Read-only tags have no interaction. Titles stay under 20 characters. Truncate at the start, middle or end with the full text on hover and focus, and never wrap. Don't use tags as links or give them several functions.
- [Carbon – Notification usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/notification/usage/): Inline notifications never auto-dismiss and sit at the top of the content area or near the item. Each status pairs a color with an icon. Body is 1–2 short sentences, no more than 2 lines, with 'View more' for longer text. Callouts are persistent and placed…
- [Carbon – Empty states pattern](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/empty-states-pattern/): Three types: no data, user action, error/permission. Each has a short title, a body giving the next step, and an optional single primary action. Plain language, no dead ends. A no-permission state says how to get access.
- [Carbon – Status indicator pattern](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/status-indicator-pattern/): Combine color, shape and symbol with a label (the page says 2 in one place and 3 in another). Fixed color meanings: red error, orange serious warning, yellow warning, green success, blue info/progress, grey not started. Don't reuse a shape with different…
- [Carbon – Spacing overview](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/spacing/overview/): Scale: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 160px. 'Space can also be used to denote groups of associated information' without dividers.
- [Carbon – Typography overview](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/typography/overview/): Use the productive type set in product UIs (condensed). Scale: 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24… Three weights (300/400/600); semibold is for section headers, not long text.
- [Carbon – Dropdown usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/dropdown/usage/): Option text is 'brief, accurate, and not descriptive'. Avoid multi-line options; truncate with an ellipsis and a tooltip. Use a combo box for very long lists, and no dropdown for 2 options. Helper text sits under the label.
- [Carbon – Code snippet usage](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/code-snippet/usage/): An inline snippet copies when clicked anywhere and confirms with 'Copied to clipboard'. Inline snippets should not span multiple lines.
- [GOV.UK Design System – Summary list](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/summary-list/): A list of key facts built on dl (key, value, optional action). Not for tabular data. Row borders help zoom users ('think carefully before you remove row borders'). Use the card variant for several lists.
- [GOV.UK Design System – Details](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/details/): For information only some users need, never what most need. The summary text is short and descriptive. Less prominent than tabs or accordions. Built on native details/summary.
- [GOV.UK – Writing guidelines: clear structure](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-structure/): Most important first (inverted pyramid). 'Do not repeat the summary in the first paragraph.' Headings are descriptive and put the key point first. People read 20–28% of a page, often in an F-pattern.
- [GOV.UK – Writing guidelines: clear language](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/): Split sentences over 25 words; paragraphs have at most 5 sentences. Specialists prefer plain English too. Explain technical terms at first use. Use the active voice.
- [NHS service manual – How we write](https://service-manual.nhs.uk/content/how-we-write): Sentences up to 20 words, paragraphs up to 3 sentences. Plain English first with the medical term in brackets. Use subheadings and bullets, and the active voice.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html): Sticky headers and footers must not entirely hide a focused component (failure F110). Technique C43: CSS scroll-padding.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html): Custom tooltips and popups must be dismissible without moving focus, hoverable and persistent.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html): Targets are at least 24×24 CSS px, with spacing and inline exceptions.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html): 3:1 for anything that identifies a component or its state (focus, selected) and for the graphic parts needed to read content. Chart lines need 3:1 against the background.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 1.4.1 Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html): Color is never the only means of conveying meaning. Red errors and red/green states need a second cue. Links need a cue beyond color.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text (18pt, or 14pt bold). Inactive components are exempt, but a refusal message next to them is not.
- [WCAG 2.2 Understanding 2.4.7 Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html): The focus indicator must be visible. F78: styling outlines or borders so the focus indicator disappears is a failure.
- [WAI-ARIA APG – Tabs pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/): A tablist contains tabs. Label it with aria-labelledby or aria-label. Arrow, Home and End keys move between tabs. aria-selected marks the active tab, and each tab is paired with its tabpanel.
- [WAI-ARIA APG – Listbox with grouped options](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/listbox/examples/listbox-grouped/): Markup: role=listbox, then role=group with aria-labelledby pointing at the group label, then role=option. aria-activedescendant tracks the active option. Supports arrows, Home/End and type-ahead.
- [MDN – ARIA tablist role](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Roles/tablist_role): Every tablist must have tab children; MDN lists no other permitted children. Use aria-labelledby when a visible label exists, otherwise aria-label.
- [MDN – Popover API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Popover_API): The popover attribute (auto/hint/manual) with popovertarget renders in the top layer. Baseline 2025 (newly available).
- [PatternFly – Navigation design guidelines](https://www.patternfly.org/components/navigation/design-guidelines): Grouped navigation shows items 'persistently beneath a non-interactive group header'. Use horizontal navigation when there are fewer than 5 primary items.
- [PatternFly – Description list design guidelines](https://www.patternfly.org/components/description-list/design-guidelines): For terms and definitions about one item on detail pages. Horizontal (inline) or vertical layout, optional columns. Term help is a popover marked by a dotted underline rather than icons, which avoids clutter.
- [Primer – NavList](https://primer.style/components/nav-list): Group titles render as semantic headings (h3 by default) with a divider before each group. Use dividers alone for purely visual sections.
- [Microsoft – Windows 7 Ribbons (UX guide)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/uxguide/cmd-ribbons) (search result only): Ribbon tabs are made of labelled groups of related commands; separators mark strongly related commands. Group names must be specific. At most 7 groups.
- [Atlassian – Lozenge usage](https://atlassian.design/components/lozenge/usage): Lozenges are non-interactive. Semantic colors carry shared meaning. Don't use color alone. Labels truncate at 200px, and because lozenges can't be focused the truncated text is never visible.
- [SAP Fiori for Android – M3 Tabs usage (Material 3 derivative)](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-android/v25-4/components/m3-standard-components/tabs/usage): Primary tabs hold the main destinations; secondary tabs sit inside a content area one level deeper. Don't use tabs for content on different levels.
- [Inclusive Components – Tooltips & Toggletips](https://inclusive-components.design/tooltips-toggletips/): Toggletip: a button plus a role=status live region filled on click, closed on Esc, blur or an outside click. Don't rely on the title attribute.
- [OWASP – DOM based XSS Prevention Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/DOM_based_XSS_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html): textContent is the fundamental safe sink, and createElement, setAttribute (safe attributes only) and appendChild are safe ways to build UI. Avoid innerHTML.
- [Chrome – A customizable select (appearance: base-select)](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/a-customizable-select): Chrome 135 keeps rich HTML inside option elements under appearance: base-select. Other browsers fall back to plain options.
- [uPlot docs](https://github.com/leeoniya/uPlot/tree/master/docs): width/height cover the plot area, axes and ticks but not the title or legend. setSize() updates the size. Axis size:'auto' (demos/axis-autosize.html) and gap are configurable.
- [Gradio – Custom HTML components guide](https://www.gradio.app/guides/custom-HTML-components): gr.HTML supports html_template, css_template, js_on_load (element, props, trigger, watch, server), server_functions and head. Listeners in js_on_load attach once, so delegate from a parent. Untrusted input in templates is an XSS risk.
- [gradio-app/gradio PR #9521 – Allow info= to render markdown](https://github.com/gradio-app/gradio/pull/9521): info= renders markdown. The installed 6.30 Dropdown docstring confirms: 'Supports markdown / HTML syntax'.
