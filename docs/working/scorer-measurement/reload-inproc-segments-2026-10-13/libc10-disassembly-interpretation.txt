Read-only AArch64 disassembly interpretation

mi_arenas_try_purge.part.0
0x4dde1c: call _mi_clock_now; 0x4dde20 preserves now in x23.
0x4dde24-2c: load global mi_arenas_purge_expire into x0.
0x4dde34: force=true bypasses ordinary deadline check.
0x4dde38-40: cmp x0,0; ccmp x23,x0,#0,ne; b.gt 0x4de11c.
For nonzero expiry: signed now > expiry branches to the epilogue/return.
For zero expiry: NZCV=0 makes GT true; also returns.
Thus ordinary overdue purges are skipped: the pre-acd6f6c direction.

0x4ddef4: loads per-arena expiry.
0x4ddf00-08: zero or not-yet-due per-arena expiry skips that arena.
0x4dded0-d8: advance to next arena; reaching the end branches 0x4de0fc.
0x4de0fc-104: store zero into the global expiry, even if all individual
arenas were skipped for being not-yet-due: the pre-4e50cec behavior.
These are verified code paths; no claim is made that their runtime state
was captured during the original reload runs.

_mi_arena_free
0x4df41c-430 reads purge_delay and arena_purge_mult and multiplies them.
0x4df438-448 checks preloading or zero delay. With zero delay it reaches
0x4df44c-458, which calls mi_arena_purge directly, bypassing the scheduler.
Positive delay in normal operation branches to 0x4df500, scheduling expiry.
